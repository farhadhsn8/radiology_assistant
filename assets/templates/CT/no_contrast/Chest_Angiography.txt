Chest CT Angiography (Aortic CT angiography)
Technique
CT angiography of the thoracic aorta was performed in the arterial phase after intravenous contrast. Axial, coronal and sagittal reconstructions were reviewed.
Findings
•	Thoracic aorta: Normal caliber and homogeneous opacification of the ascending aorta, aortic arch and descending thoracic aorta. No dissection flap, aneurysm or traumatic injury.
•	Arch branches: Brachiocephalic, left common carotid and left subclavian arteries are unremarkable. A left vertebral artery arises directly from the aortic arch (common anatomic variant).
•	Mediastinum: No mediastinal lymphadenopathy or hematoma.
•	Lungs and pleura: Lungs are clear; no pleural effusion or pneumothorax.
•	Bones: No fractures of the ribs, sternum or thoracic spine; alignment of the thoracic spine is intact.
•	Upper abdomen: Visualized upper abdominal structures are within normal limits.
Impression
Normal CT angiography of the thoracic aorta. No evidence of aortic dissection, aneurysm or intramural hematoma.
