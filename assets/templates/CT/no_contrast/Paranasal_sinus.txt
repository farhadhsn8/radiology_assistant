Paranasal sinus CT (non contrast)
Technique
•	Fine slice non contrast helical CT of the paranasal sinuses with bone and soft tissue reconstructions.
Findings
•	All paranasal sinuses are clear with no mucoperiosteal thickening, mucous retention cysts or air–fluid levels.
•	Osteomeatal complexes are patent and have a normal appearance.
•	The nasal septum is midline; nasal conchae have normal mucosa.
•	Orbits, nasopharynx and visualized oropharynx appear normal.
•	No bony abnormality; the alveolar rim of the maxilla is normal.
•	No soft tissue abnormality is seen.
Impression
•	Normal CT of the paranasal sinuses.
