Cervical CT angiography (CTA neck)
Technique
•	Post contrast fine slice helical CT images were obtained from the skull base to the thoracic inlet; soft tissue and bone reconstructions were performed.
•	Intravenous contrast was administered for angiographic assessment; multiplanar images were reconstructed to evaluate carotid and vertebral arteries.
Findings
•	The neck compartments are well delineated; the pharyngeal and glottic regions, parapharyngeal fat space, and tonsils are normal.
•	Parotid, submandibular and thyroid glands have normal appearances.
•	Muscles and tendons of the neck are unremarkable; no lymphadenopathy; lung apices clear.
•	No bony abnormality; the craniocervical junction and spinal canal appear patent.
•	The common carotid arteries, carotid bulbs, internal and external carotids show normal calibre and opacification; no atheroma, stenosis, aneurysm or dissection is identified.
•	The jugular veins opacify normally.
•	The aortic arch, carotid and vertebral arteries show normal opacification with no evidence of dissection, significant stenosis or vascular malformation.
Impression
•	Normal CT angiography of the neck; no vascular abnormality or soft tissue mass.
