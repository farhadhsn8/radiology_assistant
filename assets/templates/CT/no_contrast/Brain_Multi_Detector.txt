Brain Multi Detector CT (Non contrast)
Technique
Non contrast helical MDCT of the brain was performed with thin slice axial acquisitions from skull base to vertex. Images were reconstructed in soft tissue and bone windows.
Findings
•	Brain parenchyma: Normal grey–white matter differentiation; no acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass lesion or abnormal enhancement. Basal ganglia, thalami, cerebellum and brainstem are normal.
•	Ventricles and CSF spaces: Ventricular size and configuration are normal; no mid line shift. The sulcal and basal cistern spaces are patent.
•	Bone structures: Skull vault and cranial base are intact; no fracture or destructive lesion.
•	Orbits, paranasal sinuses and mastoid air cells: Appear unremarkable.
Impression
Non contrast MDCT of the brain shows no acute intracranial abnormality. The ventricles and sulci are appropriate for age and no mass effect, hemorrhage or mid line shift is seen.
