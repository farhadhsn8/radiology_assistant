Brain MDCT without contrast – evaluation of cerebral atrophy
Technique
•	Non contrast multidetector CT head performed using helical volumetric acquisition from the cervical spine (C2) to the vertex. Slice thickness <1 mm with 0.5 mm increments; multiplanar reconstructions in axial, sagittal and coronal planes for both brain and bone windows.
•	Images were reviewed in brain and bone windows to assess cortical and central atrophy.
Findings
•	Generalized cortical and central atrophy is seen: the cerebral sulci are markedly prominent and the ventricles are enlarged (ventriculomegaly) without bulging of the third ventricular recesses.
•	Changes are symmetrical and consistent with age related cerebral atrophy; white matter hypoattenuation is noted, in keeping with chronic small vessel ischemic disease.
•	No acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect or hydrocephalus.
•	Skull vault and visualized orbits are intact; no calvarial fracture.
Impression
•	Generalized cerebral atrophy with ventriculomegaly and prominent sulci.
•	White matter hypoattenuation consistent with chronic small vessel ischemic change.
•	No acute intracranial abnormality.
