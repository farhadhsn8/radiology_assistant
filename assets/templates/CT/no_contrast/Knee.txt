Knee CT (non contrast)
Technique
•	Non contrast CT of the knee performed with thin slice bone and soft tissue reconstructions.
Findings
•	No fracture is identified; joint alignment is normal.
•	An incidental fabella is seen in the lateral head of the gastrocnemius, representing a normal accessory ossicle.
•	Visualised distal femur, proximal tibia and patella have normal morphology.
•	No lytic or sclerotic lesions and no joint effusion.
Impression
•	Essentially normal CT knee with incidental fabella.
