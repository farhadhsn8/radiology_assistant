Pelvic musculoskeletal CT (bony pelvis)
Technique – Non contrast fine slice helical CT of the pelvis was performed from the iliac crests to just below the lesser trochanters. Bone and soft tissue windows were reconstructed.
Findings
•	Osseous structures: The pelvic ring (ilium, ischium and pubis), sacrum and coccyx show normal alignment and no fractures or bone changes. Acetabula and femoral heads are congruent without cortical irregularity or subchondral lucency. Sacro iliac joints and symphysis pubis are intact. There is no lytic, sclerotic or expansile lesion, and bone mineralization appears normal.
•	Hip joints and periarticular soft tissues: Hip joint spaces are preserved. There is no effusion, periarticular calcification or evidence of avascular necrosis. Surrounding muscles (gluteal, iliopsoas and adductor compartments) are unremarkable; no haematoma or soft tissue mass.
•	Other structures: Visualized urinary bladder, prostate or uterus and rectum are normal. No free intrapelvic fluid or gas is seen. Lower lumbar spine and sacral neural foramina are unremarkable.
Impression – Normal appearance of the bony pelvis with no fractures or degenerative changes. No acute musculoskeletal pathology is identified.
