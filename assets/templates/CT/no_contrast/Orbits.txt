Orbits CT (MSK)
Technique – Thin section non contrast helical CT of the orbits was obtained in axial and coronal planes with bone and soft tissue window reconstructions. Imaging included the anterior cranial fossa and mid face.
Findings
•	Globes and intraocular contents: Both globes are of normal size and density. Lenses are intact, and there is no intraocular foreign body or haemorrhage.
•	Optic nerves and extra ocular muscles: Optic nerves are symmetric with normal calibre and course. Extra ocular muscles are of normal size without enlargement or fat stranding.
•	Orbital walls and foramina: Bony orbital walls are intact; there are no fractures or bone defects. The infra orbital canal, superior and inferior orbital foramina, optic canal and lamina papyracea are normal.
•	Vascular structures and lacrimal glands: Orbital vascular structures show no abnormal enlargement or thrombosis. Lacrimal glands are normal. There is no orbital emphysema or soft tissue mass.
•	Adjacent paranasal sinuses: Paranasal sinuses are clear and normally aerated. The nasal septum is midline and the osteomeatal complexes are patent.
•	Extra orbital tissues: No pre  or post septal soft tissue swelling, cellulitis or foreign body. Visualised brain parenchyma and skull base are unremarkable.
Impression – Normal CT orbits with no fractures, foreign bodies or soft tissue abnormalities
