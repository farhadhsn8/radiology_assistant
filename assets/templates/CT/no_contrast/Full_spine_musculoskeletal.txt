Full spine musculoskeletal CT
Technique – Thin section, non contrast helical CT of the entire spine was obtained from the skull base through the pelvis. Multiplanar bone and soft tissue reconstructions were performed.
Findings
•	Cervical spine: There are no fractures or other bony abnormalities. Vertebral bodies and intervertebral discs maintain normal height and alignment without subluxation or dislocation. Facet and uncovertebral joints are normal. The cranio cervical junction is unremarkable with a normal foramen magnum. The spinal canal and neural exit foramina are widely patent, the cord appears normal and there is no pre  or paravertebral soft tissue abnormality. Lung apices are clear.
•	Thoracic spine: Vertebral alignment is preserved with no fracture. Discs maintain normal height, and there is no destructive bony lesion or abnormal kyphosis. The spinal canal and neural foramina are patent; no paravertebral soft tissue abnormality is seen.
•	Lumbar spine: No fractures are identified. Vertebral bodies and discs show normal height without malalignment. The facet joints, spinous and transverse processes appear normal. The spinal canal and neural exit foramina remain widely patent. The visualized sacrum and sacro iliac joints are unremarkable. There is no perivertebral soft tissue abnormality.
•	Overall alignment: The entire spinal column is normally aligned; no evidence of spondylolisthesis, scoliosis or destructive lesion. Paraspinal musculature and surrounding soft tissues are unremarkable. Incidentally visualized chest and abdominal organs show no acute abnormality.
Impression – Normal appearance of the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine without fracture, disc herniation, spinal canal stenosis or other significant abnormality.
