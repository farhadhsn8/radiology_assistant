CT Angiography of the Abdomen (Abdominal CTA)
Technique
MDCT angiography of the abdominal aorta and its major branches was performed during the arterial phase after intravenous administration of contrast. Axial source images and multiplanar reconstructions were reviewed.
Findings
•	Abdominal aorta: Normal caliber from the diaphragm to its bifurcation. No aneurysm or dissection. The lumen opacifies uniformly without stenosis or mural thrombus.
•	Visceral branches: Celiac trunk, superior mesenteric artery and inferior mesenteric artery show normal origin and caliber. Hepatic, splenic and renal arteries are patent without aneurysm or stenosis.
•	Venous phase: Portal and hepatic veins opacify appropriately; no thrombosis.
•	Solid organs and bowel: The liver, spleen, pancreas, kidneys and bowel loops are within normal limits with preserved enhancement.
•	Peritoneal cavity: No free fluid.
•	Bones and soft tissues: No pathologic fracture or soft tissue mass.
Impression
Normal abdominal CT angiogram. No evidence of aneurysm, vascular stenosis, dissection or thrombosis of the abdominal aorta or its major branches.
