Facial bones CT (MSK)
Technique – Thin section, non contrast helical CT through the facial skeleton was acquired with bone and soft tissue window reconstructions.
Findings
•	Bony structures: No fractures or other bony abnormalities are identified in the facial bones. The zygomatic arch and fronto zygomatic suture are intact. The orbital rims and nasal bones are unfractured. Pterygoid plates and pterygopalatine fossae are normal. The inferior alveolar ridge, mandible and temporomandibular joints are unremarkable.
•	Paranasal sinuses and nasal cavity: All visualised paranasal sinuses are well aerated without mucoperiosteal thickening. The nasal septum is midline with no spur, and the osteomeatal complexes are patent.
•	Orbits: The orbits have a normal appearance. The globes, optic nerves, extra ocular muscles, vascular structures, infraorbital canals, superior and inferior orbital foramina, optic canals and lamina papyracea are unremarkable.
•	Soft tissues: There is no contusion, soft tissue swelling or foreign body. The visualised nasopharynx is normal.
Impression – Normal CT facial bones with no fractures or sinus disease.
