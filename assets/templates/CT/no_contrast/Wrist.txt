Wrist CT (non contrast)
Technique
•	Fine slice non contrast helical CT of the wrist with bone and soft tissue reconstructions.
Findings
•	No fractures or other bony abnormalities are identified.
•	The radiocarpal, intercarpal and carpometacarpal joints have a normal appearance.
•	No scaphoid or hook of hamate fracture; no triquetral avulsion injury.
•	Alignment is anatomic; the muscles and tendons have a normal appearance.
Impression
•	Normal CT wrist.
