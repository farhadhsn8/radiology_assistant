Abdominopelvic CT without Intravenous Contrast (Non contrast CT)
Technique
Axial non contrast MDCT images were obtained from the dome of the diaphragm to the symphysis pubis. Oral contrast was not administered.
Findings
•	Liver and biliary system: The liver is normal in size and density; no focal high density stone or calcification is seen. Gallbladder and bile ducts are not dilated. Contrast cannot assess for enhancement, so evaluation for mass lesions is limited.
•	Spleen, pancreas and adrenal glands: Appear normal in size and density.
•	Kidneys and urinary tract: Kidneys are normal in size and contour without obvious calculus or hydronephrosis. Ureters are not dilated. Bladder has normal wall thickness.
•	Bowel: The visualized stomach and bowel loops show no obstruction, perforation or pneumatosis. Due to absence of contrast, mucosal details and subtle inflammation are not assessed.
•	Reproductive organs: Uterus and ovaries (or prostate/seminal vesicles) appear unremarkable.
•	Peritoneal cavity: No free intraperitoneal gas or high attenuation fluid. Mild low density fluid may reflect physiologic fluid.
•	Bones and soft tissues: Visualized spine and pelvic bones show no fracture or lytic lesions.
Impression
Non contrast CT of the abdomen and pelvis demonstrates no acute abnormality. Evaluation for subtle parenchymal lesions is limited in the absence of intravenous contrast, but there is no evidence of obstruction, perforation or urolithiasis.
