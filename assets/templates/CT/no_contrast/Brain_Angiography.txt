Brain CT Angiography (CTA)
Technique
CTA of the head and neck was performed using a helical MDCT scanner after intravenous injection of iodinated contrast. Thin axial images were obtained from the aortic arch to the vertex and reconstructed in multiple planes.
Findings
•	Intracranial arteries: The intracranial circulation, including the anterior, middle and posterior cerebral arteries and their branches, opacifies normally. No aneurysm, arteriovenous malformation or arterial stenosis is identified. The circle of Willis demonstrates normal branching, with a dominant left vertebral artery and hypoplastic right A1 segment as a common variant.
•	Venous sinuses: There is expected opacification of the dural venous sinuses; no venous thrombosis is seen.
•	Brain parenchyma: No evidence of territorial infarction, hemorrhage or mass lesion.
•	Bones and soft tissues: Skull base and cervical spine are intact. No abnormal soft tissue mass.
Impression
Normal CT angiogram of the head. The intracranial arterial circulation is unremarkable with no aneurysm, arteriovenous malformation, stenosis or occlusion.
