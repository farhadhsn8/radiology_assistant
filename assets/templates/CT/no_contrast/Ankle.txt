Ankle CT (non contrast)
Technique
•	Plain CT of the ankle performed on a multidetector scanner; images were evaluated at appropriate bone and soft tissue window settings.
Findings
•	Distal tibia and fibula appear normal.
•	Tibio talar and fibulo talar joints are normal.
•	Calcaneus, talus, navicular, cuboid and cuneiform bones show normal size and trabecular pattern.
•	The sustentaculum tali is normal and inter tarsal joints are preserved.
•	Metatarsals are normal.
•	No fractures or lytic/sclerotic lesions.
•	Soft tissue planes are maintained; no abnormal subcutaneous fluid collection.
Impression
•	No significant abnormality in the ankle.
