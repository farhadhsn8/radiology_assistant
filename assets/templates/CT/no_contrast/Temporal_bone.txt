Temporal bone (petrous) CT (non contrast)
Technique
•	Fine slice non contrast helical CT images were acquired through the skull base, with bone and soft tissue reconstructions.
Findings
•	Both temporal bones show no focal bony abnormalities.
•	External auditory canals, middle ears, vestibular cochlear apparatus and internal acoustic canals are normal on both sides.
•	The tympanic membranes and ossicles are intact; the epitympanic recess, scutum, mastoid antrum and mastoid air cells are well aerated.
•	The facial nerve follows a normal course.
•	The carotid canal and jugular foramen are unremarkable.
•	The nasopharynx, eustachian tubes and proximal neck structures (including parotid glands) appear normal.
Impression
•	Normal CT of the petrous temporal bones
