Chest Multi Detector CT (with IV contrast)
Technique
Contrast enhanced helical MDCT of the chest was performed from the lung apices to the diaphragms after intravenous administration of iodinated contrast. Images were reconstructed in soft tissue and lung windows.
Findings
•	Mediastinum and heart: Normal cardiac size and great vessel caliber. No mediastinal lymphadenopathy or soft tissue mass.
•	Pulmonary arteries and veins: Opacify normally without filling defect. Pulmonary venous drainage is normal.
•	Lung parenchyma and airways: Lungs are clear without focal consolidation, nodules or ground glass opacities. Bronchi and larger airways are normal.
•	Pleura and chest wall: No pleural effusion or pneumothorax. Ribs, clavicles and vertebral bodies are intact.
•	Upper abdominal organs: Visualized liver, spleen and adrenal glands are unremarkable.
Impression
Normal contrast enhanced CT of the chest. The heart, great vessels, lungs and pleura are unremarkable. No evidence of pulmonary embolism, mass or pneumonia.
