Abdominopelvic CT with Intravenous (and oral) Contrast
Technique
Portal venous phase contrast enhanced MDCT of the abdomen and pelvis was obtained from the lung bases to the symphysis pubis after oral and intravenous contrast administration. Images were reconstructed in axial, coronal and sagittal planes.
Findings
•	Solid organs: The liver, spleen, pancreas, adrenal glands and kidneys show homogeneous enhancement without focal lesions.
•	Biliary tract: Intra  and extra hepatic bile ducts are normal; gallbladder is unremarkable.
•	Bowel: Oral contrast opacifies the stomach, small bowel and colon. There is no wall thickening, obstruction or faecal loading.
•	Vascular structures: Aorta, inferior vena cava and portal venous system are normal.
•	Peritoneum and mesentery: No free fluid or gas in the abdomen or pelvis. No mesenteric lymphadenopathy.
•	Lung bases and bones: Lung bases are clear; no lytic or sclerotic bone lesions.
